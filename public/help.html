Quick tips

Find anything

Press ☰ for every section of the site and a search box that covers works, stories, creators and updates.

Never miss a post

Follow creators on Chapterhive. The 🔔 bell tells you when they post, when someone follows you and what's trending.

Post anything creative

Art, comics, stories, comedy, live action films, series, memes, polls or just how you're feeling: pick a category in Compose.

Reviewed before public

Every creator post is checked by the team first. You'll get a note in the bell when it's approved, or what to change.

Find collaborators

Writers find artists, artists find animators: the collab board lets you post what you need and reply to others.

Keep your streak

Visit every day to grow your streak. Hide your green online dot any time in Account settings.
