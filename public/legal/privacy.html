Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026 · applies to God's Banditos and Chapterhive

Terms of Service Privacy Policy Creator Agreement Content Rules

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights under Zambia's Data Protection Act, 2021. The data controller is Mulenga Mwamba (God's Banditos / Chapterhive). Contact: the contact form.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: username, display name, password (stored only as a secure hash), and, if you give it, your email address and profile picture.
  • Date of birth and country: asked when you join. Your date of birth is used only to check you are old enough and to give 13 to 17 year olds a teen account with extra protection. Neither is ever shown to other people.
  • Chat: messages you send and receive, works you share in chat, and members you block. Messages are only seen by the two people in the conversation, and by moderators if a message is reported.
  • Gender: optional (male, female, or prefer not to say). Kept private and never shown on your profile.
  • Organisation accounts: the organisation's name, type, website and team list (shown on its page), and the name, role and date of birth of the person responsible (kept private).
  • Email preferences: whether you chose to get occasional news emails (off unless you tick the box).
  • Google sign-in: if you use it, Google's account ID and your email address.
  • Creator profiles: creator name, handle, bio, links, banner and the application you send.
  • What you do on the sites: works you publish, comments, likes, favourites, follows and reports.
  • Contact messages: the name, email address and message you send through the contact form.
  • Technical data: a login cookie that keeps you signed in, and basic server logs (such as IP address and time) kept for security.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal data.

2. Why we use it

  • To run your account and show your profile, works and comments (to provide the service you asked for).
  • To send emails you ask for, such as password-reset links, creator application results and replies to your messages.
  • To keep the sites safe: preventing spam and abuse, and handling reports (our legitimate interest in a safe community).
  • Where the law requires, we will ask for your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

3. Who we share it with

Only with the services that run the sites for us, under their data protection terms:

  • Render (website hosting) and Neon (database): store account and site data.
  • Cloudinary: stores uploaded pictures, video, audio and documents.
  • Brevo or Resend: send our emails.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".

Some of these services store data outside Zambia (for example in the United States or Europe). We choose providers that protect data with strong security. We will also share data if the law requires it.

4. What's public

Your username, display name, profile picture, public member page, comments, and (for creators) your profile and published works are visible to everyone. Your email address, likes, favourites and reports are private.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your personal data and content are deleted, apart from backups that are overwritten within a short period and records we must keep by law. Contact messages are kept for up to two years.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, object to how we use it, or ask us to stop processing it. Most of this you can do yourself from your profile page; for anything else, use the contact form. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Zambia.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted (HTTPS), login cookies can't be read by page scripts, and uploads are checked. No system is perfect: if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

8. Children

The sites are not meant for children under 13. If someone enters a date of birth showing they are under 13, no account is created and nothing they typed is kept. Members aged 13 to 17 get a teen account: they can only chat with other teen accounts, and adults cannot message them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We'll update this policy when how we use data changes, and show the date at the top.
