Content Rules

Last updated 15 November 2026 · applies to God's Banditos and Chapterhive

Terms of Service Privacy Policy Creator Agreement Content Rules

Chapterhive is for original creative work of every kind: art, manga and comics, animation, live action films and series, comedy, stories and writing, memes, polls and personal posts. These rules apply to works, comments, profiles and messages. Breaking them can lead to removal or suspension.

Never allowed

  • Any sexual content involving minors, or characters who look or are described as under 18. This is reported to the authorities.
  • Pornographic or sexually explicit content.
  • Stolen, traced or re-uploaded work, and pirated anime or manga (full episodes, leaked scans, illegal download links).
  • Hate speech, or encouraging violence against people or groups.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or sharing someone's private information.
  • Impersonating another person, creator or brand.
  • Spam, scams, malware or misleading links.
  • Advertising without approval: promoting any product, service, business, event, website or sponsor (including your own sponsors), or paid or sponsored posts, unless the admins have approved it first. Ask through the contact page.

Review before publishing

Every post from a creator is checked by the Chapterhive team before it becomes public. Approved posts go live straight away (or at their scheduled time). If a post is declined, you'll get a message explaining why; fix it and publish again to send it back for review.

Allowed with care

  • Mature themes such as war, death or horror are part of storytelling. Say so in the logline or description, and avoid gratuitous gore in covers and thumbnails.
  • Fan art and fan fiction: credit the original series, and don't sell it or present it as official.
  • News posts: write in your own words, link to official sources, and only use official promotional images.
  • Spoilers: keep them out of titles and cover images, and warn readers at the start.
  • AI-assisted work: say so in the description.

Reporting

Use the ⚑ Report link on any work, comment or creator page. Reports are private and reviewed by the Chapterhive team. For copyright complaints, include proof of ownership (see the Terms of Service, section 7).
