Creator Agreement

Last updated 15 November 2026 · applies to God's Banditos and Chapterhive

Terms of Service Privacy Policy Creator Agreement Content Rules

This agreement applies when you apply to be, or are approved as, an Chapterhive creator. It adds to the Terms of Service.

1. Applying and approval

Anyone with an account can apply. We review applications to keep Chapterhive original and safe, and we may approve or decline them at our discretion. Approval lets you publish works, stories and characters on your own profile page.

2. You own your work

Everything you publish stays yours. Chapterhive does not take ownership of your stories, characters, art, animation or music.

3. The permission you give us

While your work is published, you give Chapterhive a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free permission to host, store, display, resize and format it, show it in previews, feeds and search results, and feature it when promoting Chapterhive. This permission ends when you delete the work or your account (apart from copies in backups that are overwritten within a short period, and links others have already shared).

4. Your promises

  • You created the work, or you have permission from everyone who owns rights in it (including music, fonts, photos and characters).
  • Fan art and fan fiction must clearly credit the original series and must not be sold or claimed as official.
  • If you used AI tools to generate a significant part of a work, you say so in its description.
  • Your work follows the Content Rules and the law.
  • You don't advertise or promote products, services, businesses or sponsors (including your own sponsors) in your work, profile or collab requests unless the Chapterhive admins have approved it in writing first, and approved sponsored work is clearly labelled.
  • You understand that every post is reviewed by the team before it goes public, and may be declined with a note explaining what to change.

5. Moderation

We may hide or remove works, or suspend creator profiles, that break this agreement, the Content Rules or the law, including after a valid copyright complaint. Where we can, we will tell you why and give you a chance to respond.

6. Money

Publishing on Chapterhive is free during launch, and we don't take any share of income you earn elsewhere. If we introduce paid creator plans, reader subscriptions or revenue sharing later, they will come with separate written terms that you will need to accept first. Nothing changes for you without your agreement.

7. Leaving

You can delete any work or your whole account at any time. We may end this agreement if you seriously or repeatedly break it.

8. Responsibility

You are responsible for the work you publish. If someone makes a legal claim against Chapterhive because your work broke this agreement, you agree to help us resolve it.
